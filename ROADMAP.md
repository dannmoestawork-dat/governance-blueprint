# Roadmap

**Governance Blueprint** grows by adding *pieces of the blueprint* — each new tool is a new piece; improvements to an
existing one are maintenance.

The canonical, maintained roadmap lives in the wiki: **[WIKI/Roadmap.md](./WIKI/Roadmap.md)**.

**Released — Foundations:** Navigator (observe) · In-State Governor (governance audit).

**Candidate next pieces** (directions, not commitments): a model-advisory layer for the Governor (incl.
rule-vs-rule contradiction checks); a durable agent-activity feed; an owed-items tracker; a shared
Accept/Reject/Defer recommendation-ledger module; CI integrations.

Picking something up? See **[CONTRIBUTING.md](./CONTRIBUTING.md)**. Opening an issue first is suggested, not
required — and forking to chase your own direction is always welcome.
